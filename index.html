Why "Parequilib"

Every marketplace claims to serve both sides. Most quietly favor one. Parequilib — from "para," alongside, and "equilibrium," balance — started from a simple frustration: sellers undervalue what they own because writing a good listing is work, and buyers walk away from good deals because they can't tell honest listings from padded ones.

"The fairest transaction is the one where both people understood exactly what they were getting into."

That's the whole idea here. AI helps sellers describe what they're actually selling — no more, no less. AI helps buyers find what they actually need, instead of scrolling past a hundred near-misses. The 10% fee is transparent before you ever post, because a marketplace that hides its cut isn't one built on balance.

We're early. This is still a small, honest project — built listing by listing, conversation by conversation. Thanks for being here for it.
